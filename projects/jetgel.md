## What the application does
JetGel supports urban delivery and logistics operations, including pickup/delivery locations, shipment requests, vehicle selection, delivery history and account workflows.

## My contribution
- Rebuilt and developed mobile application flows using **Flutter / Dart**, Riverpod and go_router
- Organized feature-based modules and mobile service integration
- Integrated SMS login, authentication tokens, maps and location functionality
- Implemented wallet top-up flows with card / EFT options and **PayTR** WebView callbacks
- Worked on **ASP.NET Core API / SQL** services related to users, delivery and wallet workflows
- Supported iOS/Android testing and releases through TestFlight, App Store and Google Play
